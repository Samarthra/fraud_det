Do Typology proofs hold up on real records?

Throwaway prototype. It runs the six Typologies over the real seeded FraudLens records, using plain rule code rather than an LLM. The text fact is simulated by looking for "passed OTP". The question: are the proofs correct and readable, and do look-alikes stay unproved? The 19 held-out Alerts are removed. Only records from the 7 days before a Case's Alerts count.
